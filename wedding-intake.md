## Section 2 — Your Story

Write in your own voice — casual, honest, specific. These become the narrative copy on your Our Story page. Aim for 2–4 sentences per prompt; more is fine if the story calls for it.

**Chapter 1 — How you met**
- What year?
- Where and how did it happen? The more specific the better (bookshop, rainy Tuesday, etc.)
- What happened that first day or night?

**Chapter 2 — How things progressed**
- What was a defining early moment — moving in together, a trip, a pet, a running joke?
- What did you learn about each other during this time?

**Chapter 3 — The proposal**
- What year?
- What was the plan, and what actually happened?
- How did it feel in the moment?

**Chapter 4 — Getting here** *(optional but recommended)*
- How did you find your venue or decide on your wedding vision?
- Anything about the planning that captures who you are as a couple?

**Pull quote** — one line from your story that you love. Could be something one of you said, a detail that captures your relationship, or a line from the above. This will be set large and italic on the page.
> Example: *"Eleven blocks later the rain had stopped and neither of us mentioned it."*

## Section 9 — Photos

Share photos as a single Google Drive or Dropbox folder. Label each subfolder as listed below so we can match them to the right spots on the site.

```
/photos
  /01-hero          ← 1 photo — the main full-bleed hero image
  /02-gallery       ← up to 8 photos — engagement / couple shots for the scrolling strip
  /03-story         ← 1 photo per story chapter (up to 4) — label 03a, 03b, 03c, 03d
  /04-wedding-party ← 1 headshot per person — label with their first name
  /05-hotels        ← optional exterior shots of hotels if you have them
```

**For each gallery photo**, include a short caption if you'd like one (e.g. "Golden hour on the ridge" or "Sunday coffee, Brooklyn"). These appear on hover.

**Photo tips:**
- Horizontal/landscape orientation works best for hero and gallery
- Portrait/square works for wedding party headshots
- Higher resolution is always better — minimum 1200px wide
